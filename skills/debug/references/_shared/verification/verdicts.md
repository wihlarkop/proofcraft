# Verification Verdicts

Use precise verdicts. Do not blur absence of evidence into success.

- PASS: criterion observed to hold.
- FAIL: criterion observed not to hold.
- BLOCKED: criterion cannot be evaluated because a required dependency/evidence source is unavailable.
- N/A: criterion does not apply and the reason is explicit.
- NOT RUN: criterion applies but has not been evaluated yet.

Report the evidence used for material claims and the boundary of what the evidence proves.
