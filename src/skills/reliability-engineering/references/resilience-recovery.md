# Resilience and Recovery

For a dependency/failure surface define:
- failure detection;
- deadline/timeout;
- retryable vs permanent failures;
- degraded behavior;
- overload/isolation where material;
- authoritative state;
- replay/reconciliation;
- restore/failover path.

High availability is not recoverability. Recovery claims require an exercise or other direct evidence that the state/service can actually be restored.
