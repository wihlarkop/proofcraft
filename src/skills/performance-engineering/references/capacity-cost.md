# Capacity and Cost

Connect demand, capacity, performance, reliability, and cost.

State:
- demand unit and forecast/window;
- capacity unit;
- observed saturation/performance relationship;
- required headroom;
- scaling trigger and lag;
- unit cost over the same scope/time period;
- SLO/reliability counter-metrics.

Do not invent universal CPU/memory utilization thresholds. Capacity targets require workload-specific evidence.
