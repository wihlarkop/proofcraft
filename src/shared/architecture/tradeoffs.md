# Architecture Tradeoffs

Frame consequential choices from drivers and observable quality scenarios. Compare alternatives against the same constraints and evidence rather than listing generic pros/cons.

For material decisions, state:
- drivers and constraints;
- candidate options;
- benefits and liabilities in this project;
- failure/operational implications;
- reversibility and migration cost;
- evidence gaps;
- decision and consequences.

Do not invent scale, latency, compliance, ownership, or traffic facts.
