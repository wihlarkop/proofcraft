# Bounded Work

Match ceremony to risk and uncertainty.

- Direct: localized, reversible change with obvious behavior. Inspect, change, verify, stop.
- Bounded: clear feature/change with a few meaningful decisions. Use compact planning and targeted acceptance.
- Substantial: cross-module, stateful, migration, or meaningful architecture work. Use explicit contracts, acceptance, and reconciliation.
- High-assurance: irreversible data mutation, authorization/security boundaries, money, critical production state, or distributed consistency claims. Require deeper failure/recovery evidence.

Depth may change when repository evidence reveals a different risk profile. Do not escalate merely because a specialist capability exists.
